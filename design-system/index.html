01 — FOUNDATIONS

The fold as a principle

Everything in ORIKA is born from a sheet of hand-folded washi paper. The identity translates craft, patience and precision — the Japanese taste for detail and the right crease.

Value 01

Artisanal

Nothing is industrial. Every element looks folded by human hands — the interface included.

Value 02

Unhurried

The pace is slow and deliberate. No movement rushes; nothing blinks or jumps.

Value 03

Earthy

A palette of soil, paper and fire. No neon, no cold — the warmth of coffee and washi.

Design principles

PrincipleHow it applies
The paper rulesSurfaces are sheets: they lift with a warm, soft shadow, never a hard or black one.
Unfold, don't cutTransitions reveal by folding and gliding. Hard cuts and abrupt swaps are forbidden.
Softened cornersRounded edges (the deckled edge of washi). No sharp 0px corner.
One accent at a timeCopper is the working accent. Hinomaru red is reserved for the finale.
Space is breath8px grid, generous margins. The empty cream is part of the composition.
Brand rule: the default background is always --clr-washi (#F2ECDD), the same cream as the scenes' background. This makes the diorama islands "float" with no visible frame.

07 — MOTION

The fold in motion

Four curves, all with the same idea: paper is lightweight and settles softly. Nothing accelerates abruptly.

--ease-fold
cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)
Default. Paper settling open.
--ease-unfold
cubic-bezier(.33,0,.2,1)
Crisp release, to reveal.
--ease-drift
cubic-bezier(.25,.1,.25,1)
Weightless glide (hover, color).
--ease-crease
cubic-bezier(.65,0,.35,1)
Sharp crease, rare use.

15 — MOTION V2

Scroll that drives the camera

The pattern that defines the product: scroll controls the time of a pre-rendered video, not the position of a page.

PatternHow it works
Scroll-scrubScroll position maps to video.currentTime, smoothed by requestAnimationFrame.
Frame-locked seamA clip's last frame is the next one's first — extracted from the real render, not the original image.
Seam crossfadeA few-frame dissolve absorbs minimal differences between clips.
Blob seekingEach clip is fetched as a Blob and played from an object URL — always seekable, no dependence on HTTP range.
LingerRemaps time so the camera settles mid-scene, exactly where the copy appears.
Lazy prefetchOnly neighboring clips are loaded; the rest come on demand.
Audio follows the same principle: a continuous bed + per-scene ambience, crossfaded with the active section.
