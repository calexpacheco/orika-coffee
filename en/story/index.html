Brand Story

Unfold the cup.

ORIKA COFFEE is a Japanese boutique coffee house. The name comes from ori (折り) — the fold. And the fold explains everything the brand wants to say about itself.

Origami · Washi7 actsFictional brand

The idea

The question behind the brand was simple: what if the journey of a coffee could be travelled?

Not a photo of the finished product, nor a timeline of icons — the whole path, from soil to cup, crossed end to end. The visitor doesn't read about the coffee's origin: they fly through it.

Hence origami. ORIKA's world is entirely made of hand-folded washi paper: every hill, every cherry, every figure. The paper isn't decoration — it's the argument.

The paper says, without needing to write it, what the brand claims about itself.
Hand-folded

Artisanal. Nothing here is industrial.

Honest matter, nothing disguised.

The seven acts

The journey is told in seven scenes. The camera never cuts: it dives into each one and flies to the next.

ACT 01 — FROM THE HILLS

It begins on a folded hillside.

A terraced hill, cherries as tiny folded red dots, pickers in conical hats, and mist in layers of translucent paper. This is where it all starts: the soil and the hand.

ACT 02 — HARVEST & DRY

Sun-dried, raked by hand.

The drying beds in accordion-folded paper, raked like a dry stone garden. The cherry becomes a bean under the sun, turned and turned again by hand.

ACT 03 — GRAIN SELECTION

Only the finest beans pass.

The table under the paper lamp, beans rolling down folded chutes, the loupe of clear film. Rigor enters the scene: not every bean passes.

ACT 04 — THE ROAST

Roasted to the first crack.

The copper drum spinning, heat in curling paper ribbons, the first crack in kirigami sparks. The beans darken across a gradient of paper tones.

ACT 05 — THE BOUTIQUE

A small shop in Tokyo.

A narrow shop, the noren curtain in two strips of washi, the wall of bags marked with the tsuru — the paper crane. The coffee is weighed to order, for you.

ACT 06 — TO YOUR DOOR

It unfolds at your door.

The bicycle weaving through the backstreet, the crate stacked in origami. The bag opens its side gussets like wings, releasing ribbons of aroma.

ACT 07 — THE LAST CUP · FINALE

Unfold the cup.

The paper kettle pouring, the water in a coiled folded strip, steam in white curls. And, at the center, the cup that opens petal by petal like a paper lotus, revealing the crema.

The signature move

Each scene unfolds as the camera arrives and refolds as it leaves.

Tone of voice

Unhurried

Short sentences, no urgency. The brand doesn't shout or manufacture scarcity.

Concrete

"Weighed to order behind the noren" beats "premium experience".

Never inflated

No "exceptional", "unique", "revolutionary".

One idea per line

Each scene's body copy is exactly one sentence.

The palette as narrative

Each color has a role in the story — not decoration.
